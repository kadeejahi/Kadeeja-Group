# Working Agreements

## Purpose

Team working agreements will define how the team will collaborate throughout the CampusConnect project. These agreements are intended to create a respectful, predictable, and productive development environment while ensuring that everyone has an opportunity to contribute.

## Expectations

1. Communication

- We will communicate primarily through text message and our weekly Zoom meetings, with additional meetings added, if needed, to accomplish team goals and outcomes.
- Team members will acknowledge important messages within a reasonable timeframe (24-48 hrs), even if they cannot provide immediate assistance.
- The team will communicate blockers and setbacks as soon as they are identified, rather than waiting for a deadline.
- Questions and concerns are welcome. Team members should feel comfortable asking for clarification or assistance when completing their assigned work.

2. Meetings

- Meetings will follow the agreed cadence of every Monday at 8:30pm CST and will include a brief overview of each member's work, clarify what needs to be done next, as well as serve as an opportunity to discuss progress & blockers.
- Team members will arrive prepared and participate actively.
- If a team member cannot attend a scheduled meeting, they should notify the group beforehand.
- Important decisions during meetings will be documented so that absent team members can stay informed.

3. Task Ownership

- Work will be divided into clearly defined tasks and goals with an identified primary and secondary owner.
- Team members are responsible for communicating their progress and informing the team of any blockers.
- Team members will ask for assistance when a task is taking significantly longer than planned.
- Team members will help one another when someone is blocked rather than treating each task as completely isolated responsiblities.
- Team members should not substantially change the scope of an assigned task without communicating with the team.

4. Git and Pull Requests

- Team members will make changes to the repository through feature branches, rather than directly on the main branch.
- Commits should provide a clear description of the change and avoid combining unrelated changes.
- Pull requests should explain what was changed and why the change was neccesary, at minimum (see template for pull requests).
- At least 2 team members should review pull requests before changes are merged into the main branch.

5. Code Quality and Output

- The team will follow the project's established coding conventions adn architecture.
- New functionality should include testing when practical before being merged into the main branch.
- Technical decisions that significantly affect the project should be communicated to the team and documented when appropriate.
- The team will prioritize maintainable and understandable code rather than introducing complexity and unnecessarily complicated solutions.
- No single team member should intentionally become the only person who understands a critical part of the system
- Team members should be prepared to explain their reasoning behind technical decisions, rather than just providing solutions

6. Requirements and Scope

- Requirements should be clarified before implementation whenever possible.
- If a requirement is ambigous, the team will discuss the intended behavior
- Changes to project scope should be discussed with the team
- Documentation should be updated when a significant change affects how the system is understood or used

7. Conflict Resolution

- Technical disagreements will be addressed through discussion and evidence rather than personal preference alone.
- When possible, the team will use project requirements, documentation, testing, maintainability, and other objective criteria to evaluate competing approaches.
- If the team cannot reach consensus, the team will identify the decision owner or use an agreed-upon voting/decision process.
- Once a decision has been made, team members will support the agreed-upon approach unless new evidence warrants reconsideration.

8. Accountability

- Each team member is responsible for communicating their availability, progress, and blockers.
- If a commitment cannot be met, the team member should communicate this as early as possible.


**NOTE**: These working agreements are not permanent and may be revised as the team gains experience working together. Also, the team will periodically discuss what is working and what could be improved. This document will be updated accordingly.
