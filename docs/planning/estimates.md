# CampusConnect - Project Effort Estimates

## Purpose

This document provides initial effort estimates for the major areas of the
CampusConnect project.

Estimates are expressed as Low, Likely, and High values because actual
development time may change as the team gains more information during
implementation, integration, and testing.

These estimates are planning assumptions rather than guaranteed completion
times. Estimates will be reviewed and updated when new evidence changes the
team's understanding of the work.

## Estimation Scale

- Low: Work proceeds with minimal unexpected difficulty.
- Likely: The team's current best estimate based on known requirements.
- High: Additional complexity, debugging, integration, or rework is required.

## Confidence Levels

- High: Requirements and implementation approach are well understood.
- Medium: Some implementation details or dependencies remain uncertain.
- Low: Significant technical or requirement uncertainty remains.
