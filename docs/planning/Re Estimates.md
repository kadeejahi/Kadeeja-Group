# Uncertainity and Re estimates | What to do in these scenarios


###What Uncertainity is highest:

**Project timeline estimates**
 ᯓ★-The exact amount of time needed for some tasks is uncertain because unexpected challenges may come during the making of our project.

 **Some Complexities**
 ᯓ★-Some things that we work on throughout the semester may be more difficult than originally expected, which could increase effort.

**Resource availability**
 ᯓ★-Team members may have other priorities they have to stick to or limited availability, which would be affecting the project's progress.


###What Evidence Would Trigger Re-Estimation: 

**A scope change**
ᯓ★-If new requirements are added or existing requirements change significantly, the original estimate may no longer be accurate.

**Actual progress differs from the plan**
 ᯓ★-If tasks are taking much longer or shorter than expected, the team should update the estimate based on performance.

**New risks or technical issues are discovered**
ᯓ★-Finding obstacles we weren't aware of the fact they'd possibly arise, integration problems, or dependencies that can impact timelines and require a revised estimate.
